>
> WHY IT MATTERS, AND WHY IT IS NOT SIMPLY A MISSING RESTORE. The suite runner snapshots the fixture dir at run
> start and restores that snapshot after a mutating suite, verifying the restore by SHA-256. That mechanism works
> on what it was given. What it cannot do is return a fixture to its COMMITTED bytes when the run STARTED from
> already-regenerated bytes. T-FIXTURE-01 already records the deeper cause: the acceptance fixture's saved bytes
> are not reproducible from one source, so the tracked asset drifts on every rebuild.
>
> WHAT THIS CARD OWES: either make the fixture generation byte-reproducible, or stop tracking the generated bytes
> and generate them on demand, or record explicitly that fixture bytes are expected to differ and verify content
> semantically instead of by hash. Any of the three is acceptable; leaving it silent is not, because "the runner
> restores fixtures" is currently being read as "the tree is unchanged after a run", and it is not.
>
> DO NOT mark done from a single re-run that happens to produce identical bytes.
>
